DI-GET: Real-Time Disaster Alerts and Community Safety System

1. Project Overview

DI-GET is a proposed disaster-safety application designed to help people receive timely alerts and access reliable safety information during emergencies.

2. Problem Statement

Delayed disaster warnings and a lack of accessible emergency guidance can increase risks to people during floods, earthquakes, fires, and other emergencies.

3. Proposed Solution

The proposed application aims to provide:

- Disaster alerts and siren notifications
- Voice-based precautionary instructions
- Video tutorials explaining emergency safety measures
- A chatbot for basic disaster-related guidance
- Emergency contact information for authorities, NGOs, and volunteers
- Information about nearby shelters and basic amenities

4. Sustainable Development Goals

- SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities
- SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being

5. Future Scope

Future development may include location-based alerts, verified live disaster data, and integration with official emergency services.

6. Current Status

This repository documents the DI-GET concept. The working application, integrations, and testing must be developed and verified before claiming a functional prototype.
